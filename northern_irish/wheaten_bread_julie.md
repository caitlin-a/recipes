# Wheaten Bread (Julie)
> Source: Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc), Julie's version

Northern Irish wheaten bread, baked in loaf tins. There's another version in this folder (John's).

## Ingredients

- medium or coarse wholemeal flour (18oz)
- plain flour (4oz)
- porridge oats (2oz)
- sugar (4oz)
- bicarbonate of soda (3 tsp)
- buttermilk (1 pint)
- margarine or butter, melted (2oz)

## Method

1. Mix everything together.
2. Line or grease 2 loaf tins and fill them.
3. Bake at 170°C for 35-40 minutes.
