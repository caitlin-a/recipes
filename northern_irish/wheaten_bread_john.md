# Wheaten Bread (John)
> Source: Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc), John's version

Northern Irish wheaten bread, shaped as a round loaf. There's another version in this folder (Julie's).

## Ingredients

- coarse wholemeal flour (9oz)
- plain flour (9oz)
- coarse porridge oats (5oz)
- bicarbonate of soda (2 heaped tsp)
- salt (pinch)
- black treacle (1 tbsp)
- honey (1 tbsp)
- buttermilk (500ml)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
2. Mix the dry ingredients.
3. Add the treacle, honey and most of the buttermilk and mix to a damp, sticky dough.
4. Shape into a ball, flatten it on a floured baking tray and score a deep cross on top.
5. Bake for 35-40 minutes, until it sounds hollow when tapped.
6. Wrap in a damp tea towel and eat fresh.
