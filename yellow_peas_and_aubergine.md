# Yellow Peas and Aubergine
> Source: Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc)

Whipped yellow peas with spicy aubergine and fennel. Serves 4. Don't overcook the aubergine: it needs to keep its shape to contrast with the peas.

## Ingredients

Split peas:

- dried yellow split peas (350g)
- turmeric (1 tsp)
- garlic cloves (3)
- sea salt (1 tsp)
- vegetable stock (750ml)
- olive oil (3 tbsp)

Spicy aubergine:

- medium aubergines (4)
- olive oil
- garlic cloves (3)
- cumin seeds (2 tsp)
- tomato purée (3 tbsp)
- harissa paste (1-2 tbsp)
- vegetable stock (450ml)
- red wine vinegar (2 tsp)

Fennel:

- large fennel bulb (1)
- salt (pinch)
- lemon juice (½ lemon)

## Method

1. Rinse the split peas and soak in cold water for 1 hour. Drain and tip into a heavy pan with the turmeric, garlic and salt. Pour over the stock, bring to the boil, then simmer for about 1 hour until almost all the liquid has been absorbed.
2. Cut the aubergines into thirds widthways, then each third into wedges. Fry in batches for about 10 minutes until golden, moving them to kitchen paper as you go.
3. Return all the aubergine to the pan, turn up the heat, add the garlic and fry for 2 minutes. Add the cumin seeds and fry for 1 minute, then the tomato purée and harissa and fry for 2 more minutes.
4. Pour over the stock and cook for 3-4 minutes until the liquid has mostly been absorbed. Take off the heat and stir in the vinegar.
5. Slice the fennel thinly (mandoline?) and toss with the salt. Massage for a minute until slightly softened, then squeeze over the lemon juice.
6. Blend the cooked split peas with the extra oil to a smooth purée, adding water if needed.
7. Divide the purée between bowls, spoon over the aubergine and top with the fennel.
