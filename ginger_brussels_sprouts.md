# Ginger Brussels Sprouts
> Source: Riverford (via Roger's Recipes, shared Google Doc)

Wok-fried Brussels sprouts with ginger. Serves 4.

Roger's notes: add soy sauce too. If you don't have crystallised ginger (it adds a touch of sweetness), use more fresh ginger and a pinch of sugar or a little honey instead.

## Ingredients

- sunflower oil (3 tbsp)
- onion, finely chopped (1)
- garlic cloves, finely chopped (2)
- chilli, finely chopped (1)
- Brussels sprouts, finely shredded (600g)
- fresh ginger, in fine strips (4cm)
- crystallised ginger, in fine strips (4cm)
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. Stir-fry the onion, garlic and chilli for 2 minutes, without letting them brown.
2. Add the sprouts and ginger and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
3. Add 3 tbsp water, cover and steam for 3 minutes.
