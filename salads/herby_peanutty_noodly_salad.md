# Herby Peanutty Noodly Salad
> Source: https://rivercottage.net/recipes/herby-peanutty-noodly-salad/

River Cottage recipe. Serves 4.

## Ingredients

- raw or roasted unsalted peanuts (75g)
- fine egg noodles or Thai rice noodles (200g)
- French beans or mangetout, or a combination (150g)
- cucumber (½)
- spring onions, trimmed (6)
- basil leaves, ideally Thai, roughly torn (about 12)
- mint, roughly chopped (small bunch)
- coriander, roughly chopped (small bunch, optional)

Dressing:

- rice vinegar (2 tbsp)
- lime, zest and juice (1), or ½ lemon
- small red chilli, finely chopped (½-1)
- garlic clove, finely chopped (1)
- soft brown sugar (1 tsp)
- toasted sesame oil (1 tsp)
- soy sauce (½ tsp), plus extra to serve

## Method

1. If using raw peanuts, roast them on a tray at 180°C for 8-10 minutes until golden. Cool, then crush lightly.
2. Whisk the dressing ingredients together in a large bowl.
3. Cook the noodles as the packet says, drain and rinse under cold water, then toss in the dressing and leave to cool completely in it.
4. Boil the beans and/or mangetout in lightly salted water until just tender but still crunchy (3-5 minutes for beans, 2-3 for mangetout). Drain, refresh in cold water and drain well.
5. Halve the cucumber lengthways and slice thinly. Slice the spring onions finely on the diagonal.
6. Toss the cooled noodles with the peanuts, cucumber, spring onions, beans and/or mangetout and the herbs.
7. Serve with extra soy sauce on the side so everyone can add their own.
