# Crispy Gnocchi
> Source: https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/recipes/crispy-gnocchi-with-roasted-peppers-chilli-rosemary-and-ricotta/

Rukmini Iyer, The Green Roasting Tin. Serves 2 generously, ~45 min. Use a very large, ideally metal, roasting tin for the crispiest gnocchi.

## Ingredients

- gnocchi (500g)
- mixed red, yellow and baby peppers, roughly chopped (500g)
- cherry tomatoes, halved (200g)
- olive oil (2 tbsp)
- bay leaves (2)
- garlic cloves (2)
- chilli flakes (1 tsp)
- large sprigs of rosemary (2)
- sea salt (1 tsp)
- black pepper
- ricotta (4 tbsp)
- parsley, chopped (a handful)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C / gas 7). Put the gnocchi in a large bowl, cover with boiling water from the kettle, leave for 2 minutes and drain well.
2. Tip the gnocchi into the roasting tin with everything except the ricotta and parsley. Mix well and spread out in a single layer. Roast for 30 minutes until crisp and golden.
3. Taste and add more salt and pepper if needed. Dollop over the ricotta, scatter with parsley and serve hot.
