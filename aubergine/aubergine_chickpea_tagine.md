# Aubergine Chickpea Tagine
> Source: Ghillie Basan, from my tagine book (also in Roger's Recipes, shared Google Doc)

Roasted aubergine, tomato and chickpea tagine with yoghurt. Serves 4.

## Ingredients

- ghee (2-3 tbsp, or 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter)
- cumin seeds (2 tsp)
- cinnamon sticks (2-3)
- aubergines, sliced (2)
- garlic cloves, crushed (3)
- dried red chillies, chopped (1-2)
- cherry tomatoes, halved (8-10)
- sugar (1 tsp)
- dried thyme (2 tsp)
- chickpeas, drained (400g can)
- preserved lemon, finely chopped (1)
- thick yoghurt (2-3 tbsp)
- lemon juice (½ lemon)
- salt and pepper
- dried mint, to garnish

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
2. Saute the cinnamon and cumin for 1-2 minutes.
3. Toss in the aubergine, cover and roast for 25-30 minutes, turning once.
4. Toss in the garlic, chilli and tomatoes.
5. Sprinkle the sugar over, cover and return to the oven for 15 minutes.
6. Toss in the thyme, chickpeas, preserved lemon and seasoning, cover and return to the oven for 10 minutes.
7. Beat the yoghurt and lemon juice together.
8. Drizzle the yoghurt over, sprinkle with mint and serve.
