# Aubergine Miso Roast
> Source: https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/recipes/miso-aubergines-with-tofu-sesame-and-chilli/

Rukmini Iyer, The Green Roasting Tin. Serves 4, ~55 min. Vegan.

## Ingredients

- aubergines, halved lengthways (2)
- firm tofu, cut into 1.5cm slices (250g)
- miso paste (75g)
- sesame oil (2 tbsp)
- ginger, grated (2.5cm)
- garlic cloves, crushed (2)
- spring greens, thickly sliced (100g)

Dressing:

- red chilli, finely chopped (1)
- ginger, grated (2cm)
- garlic cloves, grated (2)
- limes (2, zest of 1 and juice of both)
- soy sauce (2 tbsp)
- sesame oil (2 tbsp)
- spring onions, thinly sliced (3)

To serve:

- sesame seeds (30g)
- white rice

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan (200°C / gas 6). Cut a deep crosshatch into the cut side of each aubergine half and put them in a roasting tin with the tofu.
2. Mix the miso with the sesame oil, ginger and garlic. Rub it over the aubergine and tofu and roast for 25 minutes.
3. Toss the spring greens in 1 tbsp sesame oil, add them to the tin and roast for another 20 minutes.
4. While that cooks, mix everything for the dressing. Pour it over the aubergine and tofu as soon as they come out of the oven, scatter with sesame seeds and serve with rice.
