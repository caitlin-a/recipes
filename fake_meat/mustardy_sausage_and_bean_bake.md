# Mustardy Sausage and Bean Bake
> Source: Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc)

Serves 6. A one-pot bake with a sliced potato top. I use veggie sausages (the original says vegetarian or meaty).

## Ingredients

- sunflower oil (2 tbsp)
- large onions, chopped (2)
- veggie sausages, cut into chunks (12)
- kidney beans in chilli sauce (2 x 420g cans)
- vegetable stock (300ml)
- brown sugar (1 tbsp)
- wholegrain mustard (4 tsp)
- potatoes, thinly sliced (675g)
- butter (50g??, unit not given in the original)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
2. Fry the onions and sausages in a flameproof casserole.
3. Add the kidney beans and their sauce, the stock, sugar and half the mustard. Bring to the boil.
4. Arrange the potato slices overlapping on top and dot with butter.
5. Cover and bake for 1 hour, until the potato is tender.
6. Dot with more butter and the rest of the mustard, then bake or grill until browned.
