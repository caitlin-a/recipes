# Prawn Curry
> Source: Good Food magazine, September 2009 (via Roger's Recipes, shared Google Doc)

Prawn, pea and tomato curry. Serves 4, prep 5 min, cook 15 min. A speedy seafood curry.

## Ingredients

- vegetable oil (1 tbsp)
- onions, halved, each cut into 6 wedges (2)
- ripe tomatoes, each cut into 8 wedges (6)
- fresh root ginger, chopped (large knob)
- garlic cloves, roughly chopped (6)
- curry paste (3 tbsp; the original used Patak's tikka masala paste)
- shelled raw king prawns (400g)
- frozen peas (250g)
- coriander, leaves chopped (small bunch)
- basmati rice or chapatis, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the onions over a medium heat for about 5 minutes, until soft and starting to brown. Meanwhile, set aside 8 of the tomato wedges and whizz the rest in a food processor with the ginger and garlic.
2. Add the curry paste to the pan for 30 seconds. Stir in the whizzed tomato mix and the reserved wedges and bubble over a high heat for 5 minutes, stirring so the sauce doesn't catch.
3. Add the prawns and peas and simmer until the prawns are pink and cooked through. Scatter with coriander and serve with rice or chapatis.
