# Cinnamon Rolls
> Source: https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/best-cinnamon-rolls/

Found in Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc). Makes 9, prep about 1 hr 30 min (includes rising), cook 20 min. Quantities are in cups, as in the original.

## Ingredients

Dough:

- warm milk, 40°C (¾ cup)
- active yeast (2¼ tsp, a ¼-ounce package)
- granulated sugar (¼ cup)
- egg plus 1 egg yolk, at room temperature
- unsalted butter, melted (¼ cup)
- bread flour (3 cups)
- salt (¾ tsp)

Filling:

- dark brown sugar (⅔ cup)
- ground cinnamon (1½ tbsp)
- unsalted butter, softened (¼ cup)

Cream cheese frosting:

- cream cheese, softened (4 oz)
- unsalted butter, softened (3 tbsp)
- powdered sugar (¾ cup)
- vanilla extract (½ tsp)

## Method

1. Put the warm milk in the bowl of an electric mixer and sprinkle the yeast on top. It should foam after a few minutes. If it doesn't, start again (the milk was too hot or too cold).
2. Add the sugar, egg, egg yolk and melted butter and mix well. Stir in the flour and salt with a wooden spoon until a dough forms.
3. Knead on a well-floured surface for 8-10 minutes.
4. Put the dough in a well-oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap and a warm towel and leave to rise for 1 to 1½ hours, until doubled in size.
5. Roll the dough out on a well-floured surface into a 14x9 inch rectangle. Spread the softened butter over it, leaving a ¼ inch margin along the far side.
6. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon, sprinkle over the butter and gently rub it in. Roll up tightly from the 9 inch side, seam side down, sealing the edges as best you can. Cut into 1 inch sections to get 9 large pieces.
7. Put the rolls in a greased 9x9 inch cake tin (lined with parchment in case filling leaks). Cover with plastic wrap and a warm towel and let them rise again for 30 minutes.
8. Heat the oven to 180°C. Bake for 20-25 minutes until just slightly golden at the edges. Underbake them a little so they stay soft in the middle. Cool for 5 minutes.
9. Beat the cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar and vanilla until smooth and fluffy. Spread over the rolls and serve straight away.
