# Easy Vegan Tacos
> Source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chargrilled-vegetable-tacos-smoky-salsa

Sara Buenfeld, Good Food magazine, March 2018. Serves 2 (makes 4 tortillas), prep 10 min, cook 30 min. Vegan. Homemade wholemeal tortillas with a smoky kiwi and tomato salsa.

## Ingredients

- baby corn (175g pack)
- large red onion, sliced (1, 190g)
- red pepper, deseeded and roughly chopped (1)
- cumin seeds (½ tsp)
- olive oil (2 tsp)
- large ripe kiwi, halved lengthways (1, 110g)
- large tomato, halved (1, 115g)
- wholemeal flour, plus extra for rolling (100g)
- large garlic clove (1)
- fresh coriander, chopped (15g)
- vegan bouillon powder (1 tsp)
- smoked paprika (½ tsp)
- red cabbage, finely shredded (85g)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C / gas 7). Toss the baby corn, red onion and red pepper with the cumin seeds and oil in a large shallow roasting tin. Put the kiwi and tomato on one side of the tin and roast for 20 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, mix 60ml water into the flour with a knife to make a dough and knead briefly until smooth. Cut into 4, roll each piece into a 16cm round and cover with a tea towel so they don't dry out.
3. Take the kiwi and tomato out of the tin and put the veg back in the oven for 10 minutes. Peel the kiwi and scoop the flesh into a bowl with the tomato, garlic, half the coriander, bouillon and paprika. Blitz with a hand blender until smooth to make the salsa.
4. Dry-fry the tortillas one at a time in a hot non-stick pan, about a minute on one side and 10 seconds on the other, until they puff up a little. Spread each with salsa, top with cabbage and the roasted veg, scatter over the rest of the coriander and add a bit more salsa. Eat with your hands.
