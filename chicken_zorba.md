# Chicken Zorba
> Source: Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc)

Serves 4. Prep 15 min, cook 15 min, plus marinating. Lime and cumin marinated chicken in pitta with a hummus and yoghurt dip. I make it with Quorn chicken pieces instead of chicken. Rather than marinating for an hour, I defrost the Quorn in the microwave and let it soak in the marinade for a bit.

## Ingredients

- limes, juiced (2)
- ground coriander (2 level tsp)
- ground cumin (2 level tsp)
- turmeric (1 level tsp)
- chopped mint (1 tbsp)
- salt (pinch)
- Quorn chicken pieces, instead of the 4 chicken breasts in the original
- olive oil (2 tbsp)
- hummus (150g)
- Greek yoghurt (150g)
- pitta breads
- salad, to serve

## Method

1. Mix the lime juice, coriander, cumin, turmeric, salt and mint in a bowl.
2. Coat the chicken (cut into thin strips) in the mixture, cover and marinate for an hour if you can, stirring now and then. With Quorn: defrost it in the microwave and soak it in the marinade for a bit instead.
3. Mix the yoghurt and hummus in equal amounts and chill.
4. Heat the oil in a wok and cook the chicken for 8-10 minutes, stirring regularly, until golden (that's the time for real chicken).
5. Warm the pittas under a hot grill and split them. Fill with the chicken, salad and the hummus and yoghurt mix.
