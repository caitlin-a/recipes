# Fifteens
> Source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/fifteens

Northern Irish fridge cake, called Fifteens for obvious reasons: look at the ingredients list. A Roger Adams classic. Makes 15, prep 20 min plus at least 4 hours chilling. No baking.

## Ingredients

- digestive biscuits (15)
- marshmallows (15)
- glacé cherries, cut in half (15)
- condensed milk (about 200ml; 175ml to start)
- desiccated coconut, to coat (100g)

## Method

1. Crush the digestives in a food processor, or in a plastic bag with a rolling pin, and tip into a large bowl. Chop each marshmallow into 4 pieces and add to the bowl with the cherries and 175ml of the condensed milk. Mix to a sticky mixture, adding a splash more condensed milk if it's too dry.
2. Sprinkle most of the coconut over a large piece of cling film or foil. Tip the mixture on top and shape into a sausage about 30 x 5cm. Sprinkle more coconut over the top, then wrap tightly in the cling film and twist the ends.
3. Chill in the fridge for 4-6 hours, then cut into 15 slices and serve. It keeps in the fridge, wrapped, for up to a week.
