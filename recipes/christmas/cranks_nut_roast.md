# Cranks Nut Roast
> Source: Cranks (via Roger's Recipes, shared Google Doc)

Serves 4-6.

## Ingredients

- onion, chopped (1)
- butter (25g, a dessert spoon)
- mixed nuts, blitzed to a powder, not too coarse, as you like (225g)
- breadcrumbs (100g)
- hot stock (300ml)
- marmite (2 tsp)
- mixed herbs (1 tsp)
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. Saute the onion in the butter.
2. Combine everything to make a fairly soft, slack mixture. Roger likes to fry the nuts and breadcrumbs briefly before adding the stock.
3. Put it in a shallow dish or a loaf tin and bake at 180°C for 30 minutes, until golden brown.
