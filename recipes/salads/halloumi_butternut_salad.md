# Halloumi Butternut Salad
> Source: https://www.mobkitchen.co.uk/recipes/the-ultimate-halloumi-butternut-squash-tahini-salad

The ultimate halloumi, butternut squash and tahini salad. Serves 4. Found in Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc).

## Ingredients

- large butternut squash (1)
- red onion (1)
- halloumi (225g)
- kale (200g)
- pine nuts (100g)
- pomegranate seeds (80g)
- bunch of mint (30g)
- tahini (3 tbsp)
- garlic clove (1)
- lemon (1)
- olive oil
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
2. Cut the squash into cubes and the red onion into chunks. Put them on a baking tray, drizzle with olive oil, season and roast for 30 minutes until caramelised and soft.
3. Make the dressing: mix the tahini, half a clove of grated garlic, the juice of the lemon, salt, pepper and 1 tbsp olive oil. Add warm water until thick and creamy.
4. Toast the pine nuts in a dry frying pan, watching them closely so they don't burn.
5. Steam the kale for about 8 minutes until cooked through.
6. Cut the halloumi into thick slices and cook in a hot griddle pan with a drizzle of oil for 3 minutes on each side.
7. Put the squash, onion, pine nuts, kale, pomegranate seeds and a handful of chopped mint in a salad bowl and mix. Pour over the tahini dressing and mix again.
8. Top with the halloumi, a few extra pine nuts, pomegranate seeds and mint leaves.
