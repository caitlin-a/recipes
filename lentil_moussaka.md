# Lentil Moussaka
> Source: Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc)

Recommended by Roger. Stripy lentil moussaka. Serves 4.

## Ingredients

Lentil base:

- olive oil (1 tbsp)
- large onion, chopped (1)
- garlic cloves, crushed (2)
- green lentils (250g??, unit not given in the original)
- tinned tomatoes (400g can)
- vegetable stock (450ml)
- ground cinnamon (½ tsp)
- nutmeg (¼ tsp)
- bay leaves (2)
- sugar (2 tsp)

Topping:

- medium aubergine, sliced lengthways (1)
- courgettes, diagonally sliced (2) (?)
- olive oil (2 tbsp)
- butter (25g)
- plain flour (25g)
- milk (450ml)
- natural yoghurt (75ml)
- nutmeg (pinch)
- Parmesan, grated (3 tbsp)

## Method

1. Fry the onion until golden.
2. Add the garlic, lentils and the rest of the base ingredients and bring to the boil. Simmer for 40 minutes, adding extra stock if needed, until the lentils are soft.
3. Arrange the aubergine and courgette slices on a grill pan, drizzle with oil and grill until softened and browned.
4. Melt the butter, add the flour, then the milk, to make a thick, smooth sauce.
5. Stir in the yoghurt and nutmeg.
6. Put the lentil mixture in a lasagne dish and lay the courgette and aubergine on top in alternating strips.
7. Pour the sauce over and top with Parmesan.
8. Cook at 200°C for about 30 minutes.
