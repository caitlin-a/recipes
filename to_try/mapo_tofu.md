# Mapo Tofu
> Source: https://www.theguardian.com/food/2020/jan/22/how-to-cook-the-perfect-mapo-tofu-recipe-felicity-cloake

Felicity Cloake's perfect mapo tofu, from Roger's Recipes (shared Google Doc). Serves 2, prep 10 min, cook 7 min. Not tried yet. Uses a little meat mince.

## Ingredients

- soft or medium tofu, cut into roughly 2.5cm chunks (250g)
- Sichuan peppercorns (1 tbsp)
- neutral oil (2 tbsp)
- beef or pork mince (40g)
- garlic cloves, peeled and finely chopped (2)
- grated ginger (1 tbsp)
- spring onions, whites and greens separated, sliced on the diagonal into roughly 2cm lengths (4)
- Sichuan chilli bean paste, also called doubanjiang or toban djan (1 heaped tbsp)
- fermented black beans, drained (½ tbsp)
- chilli flakes, drained if in oil (1 tsp; you can use some on top)
- water (85ml)
- cornflour, mixed to a paste with 1 tbsp cold water (2 tsp)

## Method

1. Lay all the ingredients out next to the cooker before you start.
2. Simmer the tofu in lightly salted boiling water for a minute, then turn off the heat. Meanwhile, toast half the peppercorns in a dry pan until fragrant and grind to a powder.
3. Heat the oil in a wok over a medium-high heat until it starts to smoke. Add the remaining whole peppercorns, fry until fragrant, then scoop out and discard.
4. Fry the mince until brown and crisp, then lift out onto kitchen paper.
5. Turn the heat down slightly and fry the garlic, ginger and spring onion whites for a minute or so, without letting them burn.
6. Stir in the bean paste, black beans and chilli and fry for 20 seconds, then pour in the 85ml water and bring to a simmer.
7. Drain the tofu and add it to the wok with the mince. Spoon the sauce over, disturbing the tofu as little as possible.
8. Stir in the cornflour mixture and cook until the sauce thickens. Gently tip into a serving bowl, sprinkle with the ground pepper and spring onion greens, and serve with plain rice and perhaps some greens.
